    Features

    Everything you check before you trade, in one place.

    Market breadth at a glance

    Self-host

    Prefer to run it yourself? Three commands.

    Requires Node.js 22.13+ and pnpm. The first account you create becomes the owner; AI and vector search are optional add-ons.

    1

    Install

    git clone https://github.com/oluwaeinstein007/nVestPadi
cd nVestPadi && pnpm install
    2

    Sync today's market

    pnpm nvest sync
pnpm nvest market
    3

    Open the dashboard

    pnpm nvest serve --sync-every 60

    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is nVestPadi investment advice?

    No. nVestPadi is a research tool. It summarizes market data and documents to help you form your own view, but it doesn't know your goals or circumstances. Speak to a licensed adviser before you invest.

    Which markets do you cover?

    Every equity on the Nigerian Exchange, the largest US stocks on the NYSE and Nasdaq (each labelled with its exchange), and the top 100 cryptocurrencies by market cap.

    Where does the data come from, and how fresh is it?

    NGX prices come from the exchange's public daily board, with history from afx.kwayisi.org; US prices from Yahoo Finance, with sectors from Nasdaq; crypto from CoinGecko. Stocks are end-of-day and refreshed after each session; crypto shows a rolling 24-hour change.

    Can the AI make up numbers?

    It's instructed to answer only from the data and documents it's given, and to cite each one with a number like [1], so you can check every claim. If the data doesn't cover your question, it says so.

    How do I pay, and can I cancel?

    Pro is billed monthly in naira through Paystack (card, bank transfer or USSD). You can cancel from Settings → Plan & billing; you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

    What happens to my data?

    Your watchlist, alerts and settings are private to your account. We don't sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything in it from Settings at any time. See the Privacy Policy.
