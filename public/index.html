Features

Everything you check before you trade, in one place.

Built for the Nigerian market first, where data is scattered and tools are scarce. It covers the US too.

Market breadth at a glance

Advancers vs decliners, value traded, top gainers and losers, the most traded stocks, and every sector ranked by its move.

Every stock, explained

Trend, RSI, MACD, moving averages, Bollinger Bands, volatility and the 52-week range, summarized in plain English.

A screener that thinks in setups

Filter by sector, price and RSI, so you can ask for "oversold banks" or "most traded industrials" and get an answer in seconds.

Alerts where you already are

Price levels, daily moves, RSI thresholds and volume spikes, delivered to Telegram or Discord after every sync.

Ask AI, get receipts

Answers draw on live technical data, market breadth and your own research. Every claim is cited, and the AI won't invent figures.

Bring your own research

Add annual-report PDFs, broker notes, CSVs or whole websites. They become searchable context for every answer.

Deep dive

From ticker to thesis in one click.

Open any stock for an interactive chart with moving-average overlays, performance from one week to one year, and indicator cards that tell you what each number means.

  • BUY/SELL signals from several indicators, with the reasoning shown
  • An RSI gauge that shows overbought and oversold zones
  • One-tap watchlist, alerts and "Ask AI about this stock"
  • Shareable links like /app#NGX:GTCO

How it works

Answers grounded in data, not vibes.

  1. Sync Pull the latest session for NGX and the US. Prices land in a local database.
  2. Analyze Indicators, breadth and signals are computed on demand, without a black box.
  3. Ask Your question is answered only from that data and your documents, with numbered sources.

Get started

Running in three commands.

Requires Node.js 22.13+ and pnpm. AI and vector search are optional add-ons.

1

Install

git clone https://github.com/oluwaeinstein007/nVestPadi
cd nVestPadi && pnpm install
2

Sync today's market

pnpm nvest sync
pnpm nvest market
3

Open the dashboard

pnpm nvest serve --sync-every 60
# → http://127.0.0.1:3000/app
