Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This policy explains what nVestPadi collects, why, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as possible. It's written with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 in mind.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • What you set up: your watchlist, alerts, default market, and any Telegram chat ID or Discord webhook you add.
  • Security: for each signed-in session, the browser's user-agent string, IP address and last-active time, so you can review and sign out sessions.
  • Usage counters: how many AI questions you asked each day, to apply plan limits. We don't keep the text of your questions.
  • Billing: if you subscribe, Paystack processes your payment. We store only Paystack's customer and subscription references and your plan's renewal date — never card details.

How we use it

To run your account, deliver the alerts you set up, keep the Service secure (including rate limiting and fraud prevention), and send essential emails such as verification, password reset and billing notices. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Email delivery (Resend) receives your email address and the message content.
  • Telegram / Discord receive alert messages only if you connect them.
  • Paystack receives your email to process payments.
  • AI provider: when you ask a question, the question and relevant market data are sent to the configured AI provider to generate the answer. Your account details are not included.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie, nvp_session, to keep you signed in. It's HttpOnly and expires after 30 days of inactivity. Your theme and last-viewed market are stored in your browser's local storage. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is open. Delete your account from Settings → Delete account and we remove your profile, watchlist, alerts, sessions, tokens and usage counters immediately. Expired sessions and one-time links are purged automatically.

Your rights

You can access and correct your details in Settings, delete your account at any time, and ask us for a copy of your data or to object to processing by contacting the operator of this service.

Self-hosted servers

If you use a copy of nVestPadi run by someone else, that operator controls your data and is responsible for its privacy practices.
